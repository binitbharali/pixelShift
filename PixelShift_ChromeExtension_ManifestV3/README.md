# PixelShift - Chrome Extension (Manifest V3)

## Quick Installation Instructions (3 Steps)
1. Unzip this downloaded archive into a folder on your computer.
2. Open Google Chrome and navigate to `chrome://extensions/`.
3. Toggle on **Developer mode** in the top right corner, then click **Load unpacked** and select the unzipped folder.

⚡ **You're ready!** Click the PixelShift puzzle icon in your Chrome toolbar on any webpage to scan and convert images locally!
